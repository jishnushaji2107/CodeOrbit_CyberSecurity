

\# Phishing Email Identifier

\## CodeOrbit Tech Cyber Security Internship



\### 1. Introduction

Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers use fake emails to trick people into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links, or downloading harmful attachments.



\### 2. Objective

The objective of this project is to identify common warning signs in suspicious emails and understand how to recognize potential phishing attempts.



\### 3. Sample Email Analysis



\#### Email 1: Fake Bank Alert

\*\*Sample email:\*\*

"URGENT! Your bank account will be blocked today. Click here to verify your account password immediately."



\*\*Warning signs:\*\*

\- Urgent and threatening language.

\- Requests sensitive information.

\- Suspicious account verification link.



\*\*Verdict:\*\* Likely phishing.



\#### Email 2: Suspicious Prize

\*\*Sample email:\*\*

"Congratulations! You have won a free smartphone. Pay a small processing fee to claim your prize."



\*\*Warning signs:\*\*

\- Unexpected prize notification.

\- Requests money to claim a reward.

\- No clear explanation of how the prize was won.



\*\*Verdict:\*\* Likely phishing.



\#### Email 3: Unknown Sender Attachment

\*\*Sample email:\*\*

"Please open the attached invoice immediately to avoid a penalty."



\*\*Warning signs:\*\*

\- Unknown sender.

\- Pressure to open an attachment.

\- Threat of a penalty.



\*\*Verdict:\*\* Suspicious. Verify the sender before opening the attachment.



\#### Email 4: Fake Password Reset

\*\*Sample email:\*\*

"We detected unusual activity. Click this link and enter your password to prevent account suspension."



\*\*Warning signs:\*\*

\- Threat of account suspension.

\- Requests the password through a link.

\- Link may lead to a fake login page.



\*\*Verdict:\*\* Likely phishing.



\#### Email 5: Mismatched Link

\*\*Sample email:\*\*

"Your delivery is waiting. Track your package at the link below."



The displayed link says it belongs to a delivery company, but the actual destination leads to an unrelated domain.



\*\*Warning signs:\*\*

\- Displayed link and actual destination do not match.

\- Unexpected delivery notification.

\- Unverified website.



\*\*Verdict:\*\* Suspicious. Visit the delivery company's official website directly.



\### 4. Common Phishing Warning Signs

\- Urgent or threatening language.

\- Unknown or spoofed senders.

\- Suspicious or mismatched links.

\- Unexpected attachments.

\- Requests for passwords, payment, or personal information.

\- Unexpected prizes or offers.



\### 5. Safety Recommendations

\- Verify the sender's email address.

\- Do not click suspicious links or attachments.

\- Never share passwords or OTPs through email links.

\- Visit official websites directly.

\- Report suspicious emails to the relevant organization.

\- Enable multi-factor authentication where available.



\### 6. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how common phishing indicators can help users identify suspicious emails. Users should verify unexpected requests independently and avoid sharing sensitive information through untrusted links.
